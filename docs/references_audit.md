# References audit: `paper/refs.bib`

*Audit date: 8 October 2026. Scope: the 106 entries of `paper/refs.bib` as committed in `57493f6`, plus 7 methods-section references added at the coordinator's request. Target style: natbib (`authoryear,round,sort`) with `agsm.bst` (Harvard). Keys were never renamed.*

## Method

- **Primary sources.** Every entry was checked against a publisher or proceedings record: proceedings.neurips.cc and neurips.cc, iclr.cc / proceedings.iclr.cc and OpenReview, proceedings.mlr.press (ICML, AISTATS, UAI), aclanthology.org, proceedings.mlsys.org, ojs.aaai.org, colmweb.org, link.springer.com, nature.com, PubMed/PMC (PNAS, Nature), tandfonline.com, jstor.org, jmlr.org, cambridge.org, mitpress.mit.edu, mheducation.com, WorldCat; arXiv abstract pages for preprints and technical reports; and the releasing organisation's own pages for model documentation (GitHub organisations QwenLM, meta-llama, huggingface, EleutherAI, AI-Efficiency; blog.google; developers.googleblog.com; mistral.ai; huggingface.co/blog). Direct fetches from this container were blocked for most hosts, so confirmation came from search results restricted to those publisher domains and from direct reads of GitHub/raw.githubusercontent.com. The page used for each entry is in the table below.
- **Fields confirmed.** Author list (order and spelling), exact title, year, venue, volume/issue/pages where the venue prints them, DOI and arXiv identifier. Where a field could not be confirmed on a primary record it was removed, not guessed: the issue number of Auer et al. (2002), the page ranges of Henderson et al. (2018) and AWQ (MLSys 2024), and all NeurIPS page ranges (NeurIPS shows them only in a BibTeX download that was not reachable). NeurIPS DOIs were added only where the proceedings page itself showed one (Wei et al. 2022; Towers et al. 2025).
- **Published versions.** Every arXiv-only entry was searched for a peer-reviewed version (OpenReview, proceedings and journal sites). Upgraded entries keep the arXiv identifier in `eprint`; where the published title differs from the arXiv title, the arXiv title is kept in `note`.
- **Titles when venue metadata and paper disagree.** For three NeurIPS papers the proceedings index title differs from the title printed on the paper (GTBench: "Capabilities" vs "Limitations"; Hoffmann et al. 2022: "An Empirical Analysis of Compute-Optimal ..." vs "Training Compute-Optimal ..."; Dettmers et al. 2022: "GPT3.int8()" vs "LLM.int8()"). The bib uses the proceedings index title (as NeurIPS's own citation export and DBLP do) and gives the printed/arXiv title in `note`.
- **Author lists.** "and others" was replaced by the full list wherever the list has 30 names or fewer (12 entries). It is kept only where the full list exceeds 30 names: Kadavath et al. (36), Binz et al. 2025 (40), Qwen2.5 (42 named), Qwen3 (60), OLMo 2 (COLM version, Team OLMo + 42), Olmo 3 (67), Llama 3 (559) and the Nature DeepSeek-R1 paper (more than 100). Corporate authors are used where the document is issued by the organisation (Gemma Team, OpenAI, Google, Meta, Mistral AI, Qwen Team).
- **Format for agsm.bst.** agsm sentence-cases titles, so acronyms and proper nouns are braced; a sentence-case rendering of every title was checked programmatically (0 unprotected acronyms). agsm does not print `doi`, `eprint` or `isbn` (kept for the record) and prints `url` through natbib's `\harvardurl`, which typesets it with `\textit` rather than `\url`. A URL containing `_`, `%`, `#`, `&` or `~` would therefore break or corrupt the build, so `url` is used only for web documents (all `@misc` entries) and TMLR papers, all with URLs free of those characters. Web documents carry `howpublished`, `url` and `note = {Accessed 8 October 2026}`. AAAI papers are typed `@article` (journal *Proceedings of the AAAI Conference on Artificial Intelligence*, volume(issue)) because agsm warns when an `@inproceedings` has both volume and number.
- **Parser check.** `bibtexparser` 1.4.3 plus a strict custom checker: 113 entries, 113 unique keys, all 106 original keys present, braces balanced in every entry and field, all agsm-required fields present for each type, no unescaped `& % # _` in printed fields, page ranges with `--`, no `@` outside entries, `url` fields safe for `\harvardurl`. Result: no problems. (No TeX toolchain is installed, so `bibtex` itself was not run.)
- **Keys used by the paper.** At 22:48 UTC on 8 October the sections cite 73 distinct keys, all present in the bib; the other 40 entries are used by `docs/literature_review.md` or were added for the methods section.

## Consequences for the paper and the review

- **In-text labels produced by agsm.** When two entries share an "X et al." label and year but have different author lists, `agsm.bst` disables "et al." for those entries and prints every surname (its "confusion" pass). It adds a/b suffixes only when the full surname lists are identical. With the current bib this affects `wei2022cot` / `wei2022emergent`, `zhou2023lima` / `zhou2023ifeval`, `lin2024urial` / `lin2024awq`, `wang2024myanswerc` / `wang2024lookattext`, and `liu2025mindstep` / `liu2025rational` / `liu2025quanthurts`, which will cite as, e.g., "Wei, Wang, Schuurmans, Bosma, Ichter, Xia, Chi, Le and Zhou (2022)".
